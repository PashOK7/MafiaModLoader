## Second owner-reported pedestrian teleport

This retains the second incident's evidence and limits. A later matched rendered jump was diagnosed as
a native dodge rewind in [pedestrian-dodge-20261114.md](pedestrian-dodge-20261114.md); it does not silently
relabel all earlier transform samples. Use [STATUS.md](../../docs/STATUS.md) for the current open scope.

The native car impact at 16:37 involved PID 19940, contact serial 4. The saved contact and live
trajectory agree. The initial pose gap was zero. The first physical step moved 0.0019 m.
At 234 ms, the native animation changed the sampled root by approximately 2.4 m. The physical
pelvis advanced approximately 0.1 m and the mod reapplied its render pose. The largest consecutive
pelvis displacement in the after-world CPU samples was 0.185 m.

The evidence establishes an intermediate transform rewrite. The cause of the visible displacement
remains open. At the time of the incident the recorder did not sample the submitted skin geometry.
The diagnostic added afterwards records render transform and skinned vertex range per actor and
frame. Controlled tests detected a 2.5 m pose displacement in both builds. On native Direct3D 8 an
automatic visible kill captured 116 matched ranges before contact and 160 after, with zero faults.
Draw submission data does not establish visibility through occlusion or final rasterized pixels.

The original traces are in build/pedestrian-teleport-20261114-second. See also the 16:04 note.
