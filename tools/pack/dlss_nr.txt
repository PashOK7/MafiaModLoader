NVIDIA DLSS 5 Neural Rendering and DLSS Super Resolution for Mafia
==================================================================

For personal use only: do not upload or share this archive publicly
(it contains NVIDIA's nvngx_dlssnr.dll, nvngx_dlss.dll and
nvngx_dlssg.dll, under NVIDIA's license).


YOU NEED
--------
- For DLSS 5 Neural Rendering: a GeForce RTX 50 graphics card (RTX 5060
  and up). Older cards cannot run it: NVIDIA's file refuses them.
  DLSS Super Resolution (DLAA to Ultra Performance) runs on any GeForce
  RTX (20 series and newer), with or without Neural Rendering.
  DLSS Frame Generation runs on a GeForce RTX 40 or 50.
- NVIDIA driver 617.42 or newer (nvidia.com/drivers or the NVIDIA App).
- Mafia 1.2 (Steam / GOG / patched retail) or 1.0.
- The game running on the GeForce. A PC whose processor has graphics of
  its own (AMD Radeon(TM) Graphics, Intel UHD...) or a laptop may run it
  there instead: Windows Settings > System > Display > Graphics > add
  Game.exe > Options > "High performance" (the NVIDIA GPU), and the
  monitor plugged into the graphics card, not the motherboard.


INSTALL
-------
Unpack everything from this archive into the game's folder (the folder
with Game.exe), then run Setup.exe once:
  - Fullscreen on or off: on, the game opens as a borderless window
    over the whole monitor instead of an exclusive full screen (frame
    generation shows its frames from a window of its own); a resolution
    of another shape than the screen's is shown at its own proportions,
    black bars beside it,
  - Resolution: your screen's own (2560 x 1600 on a 2560x1600 laptop).
    DLSS draws the game smaller by itself; a smaller resolution here
    only makes the picture coarser,
  - Save and exit.
Then start the game as usual.

The archives of 24 and 25 October had Ultimate ASI Loader as d3d8.dll
with a d3d8.ini: delete d3d8.ini (d3d8.dll is replaced). Ultimate ASI
Loader is dinput8.dll here, as most Mafia mods expect: the game loads
it, Setup.exe does not, so ASI mods in scripts\ run in the game alone.
If the game folder has d3d8.dll or dinput8.dll from other mods, back
them up first: this archive replaces them. Windows asks for
administrator rights for Setup.exe (it does so for any program named
"setup"): that is expected.

MAFIA FUSION FIX is part of this archive: ThirteenAG's widescreen fix
and more, ported to the loader (mml\mods\fusion_fix). Do not install
ThirteenAG's own Fusion Fix beside it: if scripts\Mafia.FusionFix.asi
or Mafia.WidescreenFix.asi is in the game's folder, delete it (and its
.ini); beside them the built-in one does nothing (mml\logs\mml.log says
why). Its settings are in the game: Options > Mods > Fusion Fix, each
applied at once. What it does, as the original:
  - the HUD, the map, the menus and the videos at your screen's shape,
    the picture wider on a wide screen (Hor+), cutscene borders,
  - the Xbox's radar with the city's map in it,
  - a free camera round the car (the mouse, the right stick),
  - the draw distance (600 m; Draw distance off gives the game's own
    back if the frame rate drops: the processor's work grows with it),
  - the moving eyes and 1.0's car scratches, a pad's dead zones.
Not in it: its savegame\settings.bin (the game keeps Setup.exe's
settings in the registry, as without Fusion Fix: if you used
WriteSettingsToFile, run Setup.exe once and press Save and exit),
ConsoleGamma (its darker "Xbox 360" curve: the game's own look stays)
and SMAA (DLSS smooths the edges itself).

This archive includes DXVK's d3d9.dll (the game's Direct3D 9 on Vulkan)
and dxvk.conf (textures filtered anisotropically, 16x: sharp at a
grazing angle): with it the game and DLSS hand each frame over on the
graphics card and the game never waits for it. Keep both. If you have
DXVK already, keep yours (2.1 or newer) or take this one; dxgi.dll and
d3d11.dll of DXVK may stay too.
ReShade 6.8 on top of DXVK (Vulkan) with its effects runs with the mod
(tested on an RTX 3070, the mod's frame path without DLSS 5 itself).

With a texture pack, DXVK or ReShade, and at high resolutions, apply the
"4GB patch" (Large Address Aware) to Game.exe: the game is 32-bit and
otherwise runs out of memory while a mission loads (a crash in LS3DF.dll).
The first line of mml\logs\mml.log says how much it has.


USE
---
F12           DLSS on / off (remembered).
Esc > Options > Mods > DLSS
  DLSS 5 NR         Neural Rendering on / off.
  Super Resolution  Off, DLAA, Quality, Balanced, Performance,
                    Ultra Performance. The game draws its world at the
                    mode's smaller size (Performance: a quarter of the
                    pixels), Neural Rendering runs there, and Super
                    Resolution brings the picture back to the screen's;
                    the HUD is drawn at the screen's size. The faster
                    the mode, the softer the picture.
  Pipelined         DLSS works on one frame while the game draws the
                    next one (the picture is one frame behind): much
                    faster. Off: every frame waits for DLSS.
  Frame generation  Off, 2x, 3x, 4x (an RTX 40 or 50): DLSS makes
                    frames between two of the game's and shows them
                    evenly spaced. The game always runs at its own
                    frame rate (frame generation never slows it): the
                    setting is the most frames made, and only as many
                    as fit under the monitor's refresh rate are made
                    (144 Hz and the game at 50 fps: 2x; at 30 fps: 4x;
                    at 80 fps or more: none, the game alone fills it).

  Picture           strength, tone, detail, model A/B/C.
  Compare           a split screen with and without.
  Advanced          the presets of Neural Rendering and of Super
                    Resolution (Default, J, K, L, M).

When a mission or the free ride starts, the message line at the bottom
left says what runs, for example "DLSS: 5 NR + SR Performance". If
something cannot run, it says why (for example "needs a GeForce RTX 50
GPU", "the game is not on the NVIDIA GPU" or "the graphics bridge is
off": the log says why).

Start with Super Resolution on Performance and go up to Quality or DLAA
while the frame rate allows it.


FPS UNLOCK
----------
The game's 62.5 fps limit is taken away, its time kept real (cars,
people and physics move as at 62.5 fps). A line at the top left shows
the frames a second.
F11           the unlock on / off (off: the game's own 62.5).
A cap of its own, and the line hidden: create the file
<game>\mml\data\fps_unlock\config.json with
  { "max_fps": 72, "show": false }
(0 for no cap). It is read again while the game runs.


THE GAME'S OWN BUGS
-------------------
The loader fixes these, always:
  - Steam's 1.2 keeps the options set in the game (level of details,
    draw distance, effects, sounds, shadows, particles) after a restart:
    it wrote them where it never reads them. The game saves them when
    you leave by its menu's Quit.
  - Steam's 1.2 keeps Sniper Mode's key: it took it away at every start.
  - In Options > Controls a key or a joystick axis the game's font
    cannot show (another keyboard layout, Windows in another language)
    is named in English.


INTROS AND VIDEOS IN ANY FORMAT
-------------------------------
The game's own videos (the logos at the start, the intro) are played
by the loader with FFmpeg: any format (MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV; H.264,
HEVC, VP9, AV1, 8 or 10 bits), any size and frame rate (4K at 60 fps
plays smoothly), decoded on the graphics card, in full colour. The game
itself only plays small Bink or AVI files, in 16-bit colour.

To replace a video, put a file of the same name with one of these
extensions in the game's folder, beside Game.exe:
  logo1.mp4   logo2.mp4   logo3.mp4   intro.mp4   (1.2)
  logo1.mp4   logo2.mp4   intro.mp4                (1.0)
.mp4, .mkv, .webm and .mov are tried first; without one, the game's
own .bik or .avi plays as before. Any key skips a video, as in the
game. When this archive has the folder mml\mods\upscaled_intro, its
intro plays instead of the game's: delete that folder to have the
game's own back. mml\logs\mml.log says which file played and how ("decoded by
D3D11VA" is the graphics card).


UNINSTALL
---------
Delete from the game's folder: MafiaModLoader.asi, d3d9.dll,
dxvk.conf, d3d8.dll, dxwrapper.dll, dxwrapper.ini, the "mml" folder
(and README.txt, licenses), and dinput8.dll unless other ASI mods need
it. No game files were changed.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Send one file: <game>\mml\logs\mml.log (the last run's; mml.prev.log is
the run before it). It holds DXVK's, NVIDIA DLSS's and the game's own
logs too. After a crash, also everything in <game>\mml\crash\.
If mml.log is missing, the loader did not start at all: then send
<game>\Game_d3d9.log and a screenshot of the error, if there was one.
